## Week 2 - Elicitation

## Stakeholders
1. Students - Availability, easy booking
2. Staff - Equipment
3. College - Purchasing/managing equipment
4. Suppliers - Equipment, alerts for limited/missing equipment
5. IT Support - Assistance for students/staff
6. Security - Data protection for the system

## Stakeholder Needs & Concerns
1. Students:

   (a) Need: To be able to book the equipment at any time with no hassle.

   (b) Concern: The fear of being unfairly fined from being unable to return the equipment back in time due to circumstances beyond their control.
   
2. Staff:
   
   (a) Need: May want to know the student that is booking the equipment.
   
   (b) Concern: More workload if they have to manually approve every student request.
   
3. College:
   
   (a) Need: Have good management over booking equipment.
   
   (b) Concern: Replacement costs for any lost/missing/damaged equipment could be expensive.
   
4. Suppliers:
   
   (a) Need: Alerts for limited/missing equipment by the purchaser (the college).
   
   (b) Concern: Delayed communication between the suppliers and the college regarding for lost/missing/damaged equipment which could disrupt their inventory supply chains.
   
5. IT Support:
   
   (a) Need: Assistance for students and staff.
   
   (b) Concern: Could get overwhelmed with support tickets if the system interface is confusing or if the authentication for users fails frequently.
   
6. Security:
   
   (a) Need: Data protection security for the system.
   
   (b) Concern: Any unauthorised access to student or staff or any data leaks that leads to the exposure of personal information.

## Unknowns
