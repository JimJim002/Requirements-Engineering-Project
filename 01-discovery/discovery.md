## Week 1 - Initial Discovery

## 1. Facts
1. Bookings are currently managed through a mixture of email, spreadsheets and informal arrangements.
2. The booking system is mainly for booking shared equipment like laptops, cameras, projectors and specialist equipment.
3. Equipment can accidently be double-booked and are not returned when expected, making it hard for people to know if said equipment is available.
4. Staff have to waste time manually checking any bookings made.
5. There's uncertainty from students who don't know who to contact about access to equipment.

## 2. Assumptions
1. Staff overview report.
2. How much equipment is there available for booking?
3. Different booking rules for staff/students and for the types of equipment.
4. How long can a person loan/borrow/use an equipment for until required to return it (booking time frame)?
5. Access to phones or computers.

## 3. Unknowns
1. Manual or automated system?
2. Staff/student numbers?
3. Consequences/penalty for not returning equipment?
4. Maintence provider?
5. Who is responsible for supplying the equipment?
6. Time limit difference for the type of equipment?
7. The budget required?
8. Accessibility requirements?
9. Reminders?

## 4. Stakeholders
1. Students - Availability, easy booking
2. Staff - Equipment
3. College - Purchasing/managing equipment
4. Suppliers - Equipment, alerts for limited/missing equipment
5. IT Support - Assistance for students/staff
6. Security - Data protection for the system
Challenge: Staff/College may want to know the student booking the equipment while students may prefer to be anonymous when booking equipment.

## 5. Goals
1. This system should help the college to efficiently monitor the amount of bookings for equipment (prevent the ability of double-booking an equipment), the availability/visibility of equipment, and any missing/not returned equipment.

## 7. Candidate Requirements
Functional requirements:
1. The system shall allow an authorised user to request equipment for a specified date and time.
2. The system shall reveal the availability of the equipment for authorised users.
3. The system shall notify the deadlines for returning equipment to users, along with the fines in failing to do so.
4. The system shall allow authorised staff to manage equipment bookings.
Non-functional requirements:
1. The system shall support accessible use.
2. The system shall restrict booking-management functions to authorised users.
